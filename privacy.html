Privacy Policy

How LP Residence collects, uses and protects your information — on our website and in our Android app.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

LP Residence is a family‑run homestay located at 62/12 Temple Road, Kottawa, Sri Lanka (near Colombo). This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website (lpresidence.lk) or our LP Residence Android app, which loads the same website.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us when you send a booking enquiry through our contact form, including:

  • Your name and country
  • Check‑in and check‑out dates
  • Number of guests
  • Any message or special request you include

This information is sent securely to us via our form provider (Formspree) so we can respond to your enquiry. We do not ask for passport numbers, ID numbers or card details through this form — please don't include them.

Website usage data

Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (pages viewed, general location, device type). This helps us improve the site. Google Analytics may use cookies for this purpose. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Payments

Booking deposits are paid directly through PayPal. We do not see or store your card or PayPal account details — that information is handled entirely by PayPal under its own privacy policy.

Other services you may choose to use

Our site and app link out to WhatsApp, Facebook, Airbnb, and Booking.com. If you use these, your interaction is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not ours.

Our Android app specifically

The LP Residence Android app displays our website inside the app. The app itself does not separately collect, store or transmit any additional personal data beyond what is described above for the website. The app does not access your contacts, camera, location, photos or files.

How we use your information

  • To respond to booking enquiries and manage your stay
  • To improve our website and app experience
  • To communicate with you about your booking, if you've contacted us

We do not sell your information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and manage any related booking, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Children's privacy

Our website and app are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.
